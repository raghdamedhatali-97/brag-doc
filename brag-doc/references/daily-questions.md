# Daily check-in questions

Ask in this order, one at a time. Keep your wording short and warm. The whole check-in should take under 3 minutes.

## The questions

| # | Question | What a good answer has |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What were the main tasks you worked on today? | 1-3 concrete tasks with a name (project, report, meeting, deliverable) |
| 2 | What was the impact? | What changed: time, money, quality, risk, a decision, a customer or team outcome |
| 3 | Who were the main stakeholders? | Names or roles and teams, and what you did with them |
| 4 | What challenges or blockers did you face? | The obstacle and what you did about it (or "none") |
| 5 | What did you learn or get better at? | A skill, tool, domain insight or way of working |
| 6 | (Optional) Anything else you want to highlight? | Praise received, a small win, help given, anything extra |

If the user answers several questions at once, take what they gave and ask only for what is missing.

"Nothing much today" is a valid answer. Record it as a light day and do not push.

## Vague-answer signals and follow-up probes

Probe at most twice per question. If there is still nothing specific, accept the answer.

### Tasks (Q1)
| Signal | Probe |
|---|---|
| "Meetings", "emails", "usual stuff" | "Which meeting or thread mattered most today, and what came out of it?" |
| "Worked on the project" | "Which part of it? What did you finish or move forward?" |

### Impact (Q2)
| Signal | Probe |
|---|---|
| "It went well", "team was happy" | "What changed because of it? Time saved, errors fixed, money, or a decision unblocked?" |
| "Saved time" | "Roughly how much? Hours per week, days per cycle?" |
| "Improved the process" | "What was it like before, and what is it like now?" |
| "Helped the team" | "Who, and what could they do afterwards that they couldn't before?" |
| No number at all | "Is there a number you could attach, even an approximate one you're confident in? If not, that's fine." |

Never suggest a number yourself. If the user gives a range or estimate, record it as they said it ("about 5 hours").

Impact types to help the user think (offer only if stuck):
- **Time:** hours saved, cycle time cut, deadline met early
- **Money:** revenue, cost avoided, budget saved
- **Quality:** errors reduced, accuracy, fewer escalations
- **Risk:** compliance issue caught, audit finding closed
- **Decision:** analysis that led a leader to decide something
- **People:** someone trained, onboarded or unblocked

### Stakeholders (Q3)
| Signal | Probe |
|---|---|
| "The team", "management" | "Which person or team specifically? Their role is enough if you'd rather not name them." |

### Blockers (Q4)
| Signal | Probe |
|---|---|
| Blocker named, no action | "What did you do about it, or what do you need to clear it?" |

A blocker that needs the manager's help goes into the weekly update under "Where I need help".

### Learning (Q5)
| Signal | Probe |
|---|---|
| "Nothing" | Accept it. Optionally: "Any small thing, like a shortcut, a person's way of working, or something about the business?" |

## Objective tagging

After Q1 and Q2, tag each task with the objective it supports (`O1`, `O2`, ...) using the Objectives file. If a task supports none, tag it `Extra mile`. If unsure, ask: "Does this count toward O2 (reduce closing time) or is it extra?"

Extra mile means **valuable work outside the user's objectives**: helping another team, a process fix nobody asked for, mentoring, representing the team, an idea that got adopted. Routine duties that simply are not listed in the objectives are not extra mile. Tag those `Core duty`.
